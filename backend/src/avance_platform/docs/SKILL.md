## Platform

Everything a person does to a project *other than have a conversation
with it*: the project editor, the graph, sources and files, revisions and
publishing, the app store, user management, invitations, Settings and
Manage services, login and the profile page.

This is the authoring side of the product. An installation without it
still holds conversations on whatever channels it was built with — it
simply offers no way to change, inspect or administer anything, and
nothing on screen suggests an editor ever existed.

**What the apps cost.** Manage services › Platform › Costs lists every
published app. Opening one shows what it cost in total and splits it into
four branches: **Test** (its benchmark runs), **Preview**, **Run** (the
editor's own test chats) and **Users** (every live conversation, together).
Users opens into the people who spent on it, and each of them into their
sessions. Selecting any node charts its cost per day over the last 30
days, one line per AI provider, priced with the providers' own
per-million-token prices, next to four totals: the last 24 hours, the
last 7 days, and the mean per day and per week since its first cost in that
window.

The app's page and the Users page also show how much a single turn costs:
how many turns there were, their mean and median, and a histogram of
what each one cost — every model call a reply made counts toward that one
turn. On the app's page each turn also carries an even share of
everything else the app spent in the window (Test, Preview, Run, work in
the editor), shown as "Extras per turn": what a conversation really costs
once the cost of building and checking the app is paid by its use.
